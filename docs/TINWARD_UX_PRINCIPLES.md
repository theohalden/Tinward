# Tinward UX principles and screen decisions

Written 2026-11-09 after walking every screen as a first-time user. The aim: a person should never have to work out what a number means or where to go next.

## Principles

1. **One screen, one question.** Home answers "how is my month going?"; Activity "what happened?"; Plan "what is coming and what am I aiming for?"; Accounts "where is my money?". Anything else is reached from one of these, never hidden.
2. **Never show two numbers that look like the same thing.** Home had "Left to spend" and "Available to spend" with opposite signs. They are now "Left this month" (income minus what you have spent) and "Cash after bills and goals" (your accounts, less what is due). Each says what it is made of.
3. **Say what a button does.** "Paid" on an upcoming bill read as a status, so it is now "Mark paid" / "Mark received".
4. **Charts answer a question.** Spending is shown as pace against last month (is this month going faster or slower?), not as raw daily bars. Every chart can be read by touch, and a month still in progress is never compared with a whole one.
5. **Short beats complete.** Row subtitles are one line ("Card payment"), disclaimers are one sentence, and the explanation lives where the user can open it. Repeated words are removed (the account name was printed three times per card).
6. **Show the user's own numbers before the theory.** The 50/30/20 tab used to be a wall of text; it now opens with this month's needs, wants and savings against target, with the explanation below.
7. **Loading is calm.** Nothing flashes. Home waits for its data and shows placeholder shapes; unlocking fades in.
8. **No dead ends or hidden doors.** Insights was reachable only by tapping the big number; Home now has a visible "Insights ›" card.
9. **Honest labelling stays.** Tracked balances are estimates, Plus items say Plus, nothing claims to be live bank data.

## Navigation decision

Four tabs and the add button: Home, Activity, Plan, Accounts. Review is a task, surfaced as a banner on Home; Insights opens from the Spending pace card; Settings from Home's header. Adding more tabs would raise the load on every screen to serve two occasional ones.

## Done so far

Unlock flash removed, lock screens centred and animated, line charts (spending pace, monthly trend) with touch reading, Home labels disambiguated, Activity rows simplified, Accounts cards decluttered, Bills actions clarified, 50/30/20 tab made live, Add transaction ordered amount-first, Plan tab "Rules" renamed "50/30/20".
